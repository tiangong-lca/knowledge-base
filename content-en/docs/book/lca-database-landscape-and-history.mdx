Table 1. Comparative matrix of eight mainstream LCA background database paradigms across governance, delivery form, system-model conventions, and licensing boundaries.

| Database Paradigm & Examples | Maintainer & Role | Scope & Paradigm | Primary Delivery Form | System Models / Allocation Conventions | Access & Licensing Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transparent unit-process libraries (ecoinvent v1–v3.11+) | ecoinvent Association (Switzerland) | Global multi-sector process LCI (energy, materials, chemicals, agriculture, transport, waste) | Transparent unit processes (UPR) and aggregated LCI results | Raw data decoupled from linking: Cut-off, APOS, Consequential, EN 15804 | Public methodology docs; tiered commercial/academic license for machine-readable data |
| Engineering-led aggregated commercial libraries (Sphera MLC / formerly GaBi) | Sphera modeling team with partner industry associations | Global multi-sector industrial supply chains (with third-party verification) | Largely vertically/horizontally aggregated LCI plus selected unit processes | Centrally governed engineering modeling and sector-specific allocation or expansion | Proprietary commercial license; internal process tables not publicly inspectable |
| Regulatory secondary datasets (EU EF 3.0 / 3.1; historical ELCD) | European Commission JRC and contracted data nodes | EU PEF/OEF compliance supply chains (ELCD no longer maintained; historical archive only) | ILCD-EF partly terminated systems | Mandatory Circular Footprint Formula (CFF) and EU Recommendation rules | Use-restricted access for PEF/OEF compliance; redistribution restricted |
| Open public federal repositories (USLCI / Federal LCA Commons) | U.S. NREL with USDA, DOE, EPA, FHWA, USFS, and NIST | U.S. domestic energy, transport, construction, forestry, and core industry | Open unit-process repositories paired with FedEFL and public API | Attributional process focus; standalone USLCI has unclosed upstream gaps | Public domain, free open access |
| National/regional libraries & open specs (IDEA, CLCD, AGRIBALYSE, TIDAS) | AIST (Japan), IKE/SCU (China), ADEME/INRAE (France), TianGong ecosystem | Domestic grids, industry, agri-food chains, or open exchange specifications | National background libraries (IDEA/CLCD), open results nested on commercial backbones (AGRIBALYSE), or open validation schemas (TIDAS) | Domestic attributional baselines; TIDAS separates public semantic contracts from consumer policy | Mixed: AGRIBALYSE results and TIDAS spec are open; IDEA/CLCD machine-readable data are licensed |
| Industry-association averages (worldsteel, PlasticsEurope, IAI, etc.) | Global and European steel, plastics, and aluminium associations | Core material routes (BF-BOF/EAF steel, polymers, primary/recycled aluminium) | Aggregated cradle-to-gate LCI protecting plant confidentiality | Association-specific recycling and co-product rules (e.g., closed-loop or value correction) | Request-based or member access; plant-level unit processes confidential |
| Agri-food and chemical specialty libraries (Agri-footprint, WFLDB, Carbon Minds) | Blonk, Agroscope/Quantis, Carbon Minds | Global crops, feed, livestock, food processing, or route-specific regional chemicals | Specialized agri-food or chemical unit and aggregated datasets | Multi-variant allocation options (economic/mass/energy) or regional technology-route contrasts | Commercial or consortium license; integrated in major LCA software |
| Input–output & social risk matrices (USEEIO v2.0, EXIOBASE 3, PSILCA, SHDB) | U.S. EPA, EXIOBASE Consortium, GreenDelta, New Earth | Economy-wide monetary–environmental matrices and trade-coupled social risk hours | Symmetric IO matrices + environmental or social satellite accounts | Industry/commodity technology projection; zero upstream truncation (see Hybrid Inventory Analysis and Social Performance and Impacts) | USEEIO open-source reproducible; EXIOBASE open for research; PSILCA/SHDB commercial |

## Geographic Residuals, Markets, and Capital-Goods Boundaries [#geographic-residuals-markets-and-capital-goods-boundaries]

<Prose ids="cl-db27-geography-and-row-residuals cl-db27-market-electricity-transport-modeling cl-db27-capital-goods-asymmetry cl-db27-cutoffs-and-temporal-lag" />

Table 2. Foundational methodological comparison of five major background database paradigms across system models, aggregation depth, geography and market modeling, capital goods, and cut-off conventions.

| Methodological Axis | ecoinvent v3 | Sphera MLC (formerly GaBi) | EU EF 3.0 / 3.1 Secondary Data | National Public Libraries (USLCI / IDEA / CLCD) | EEIO / MRIO (EXIOBASE 3 / USEEIO) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multifunctionality & recycling | Raw datasets decoupled from linking: Cut-off, Cut-off EN 15804, APOS, and Consequential | Centrally governed engineering allocation or system expansion by sector | Mandatory Circular Footprint Formula (CFF) partitioning primary and recycled burdens | Typically fixed to a single attributional convention per library/sector | National supply-use tables projected via industry- or commodity-technology assumptions |
| Delivery granularity & transparency | Transparent unit processes (UPR) enabling disassembly and Monte Carlo propagation | Largely vertically/horizontally aggregated LCI (confidentiality) plus selected UPRs | Primarily ILCD-EF partly terminated systems; minor subprocesses non-reusable standalone | USLCI open UPRs; IDEA and CLCD licensed domestic background libraries | Macro sector-level monetary-environmental matrices; no plant-level disassembly |
| Geography & residual rules | Strict non-overlapping geographies; sub-national grids for large countries; dynamic GLO/RoW residuals | Country and regional production/consumption averages maintained by one team | European and major global trade geographies bound to official EF location codes | Anchored in domestic electricity, fuel, and industrial statistics; imports often link globally | National/regional economic boundaries; USEEIO v2.0 separates domestic vs. foreign impacts |
| Transport & electricity boundaries | Transforming activities (gate) separated from market activities (embedded freight, trade, losses) | Most industrial profiles delivered at factory gate; foreground often adds transport explicitly | Combines residual electricity mixes (net of tracked renewables) and explicit transport rules | Voltage levels, grid losses, and embedded transport vary by library and require verification | Transport and electricity closed as economic sectors via purchaser-price and margin matrices |
| Capital goods & cut-off rules | Capital goods and infrastructure included by default across most processes; process cut-offs | Industrial profiles frequently exclude or selectively include capital goods | Cumulative cut-offs < 3.0% for mass, energy, and each impact category; rule-bound capital goods | Uneven capital-goods coverage; standalone USLCI historically has unclosed upstream gaps | Zero upstream economy-wide truncation (capital formation depends on endogenization) |

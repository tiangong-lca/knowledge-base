## Limits of System Reconstruction [#limits-of-system-reconstruction]

<Prose ids="cl-hist-assembly-check" />

The table below lists the two system totals published in the 1974 report and compares the published OWG total against the sum recalculated from the 13 line items displayed in Volume II Table 8. Both OWG totals are scaled to 1,000,000 fillings, yet rest on different accounting bases and must be distinguished.

| Source and Quantity | Value | Unit or Conversion Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published report: 10-RET total energy | 2,026 | million Btu / 1,000,000 fillings |
| Published report: OWG total energy | 6,036 | million Btu / 1,000,000 fillings |
| Sum of 13 displayed OWG line items | 29.102 | million Btu / short ton of system basis |
| Line-item sum × 205 short tons | 5,965.910 | million Btu / 1,000,000 fillings |
| Published total minus assembled line-item total | 70.09 | million Btu / 1,000,000 fillings |

Each filling here is 12 fluid ounces. The difference in the table equals 6,036 − 29.102 × 205; it locates an unclosed assembly relationship without establishing the cause of the gap.

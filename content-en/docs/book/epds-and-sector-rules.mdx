## Construction Modules and Cross-Operator Harmonization [#construction-modules-and-harmonization]

<Prose ids="cl-epd31-building-context-modules cl-wallext52-epd-declared-vs-fu cl-epd31-eco-platform-harmonization" />

Table 1. Governance and calculation comparison across three open European EPD rule frameworks.

| Dimension | International EPD System GPI v5.1.0 (2026-11-10) | IBU General Instructions v2.1 (effective 2022-10-01) | ECO Platform Standards (LCA Rules V2.0 & Digital Data V1.1, Dec 2024) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Referenced Standards (Open Citations) | ISO 14025:2026, ISO 14040/14044, EN 15804+A2, ISO 21930, EN 15941, ISO/TS 14027/14029 | ISO 14025:2006, EN 15804+A1/+A2, ISO 21930:2017, ISO/IEC 17029 | EN 15804+A2 baseline; ISO 21930 permitted additively when all ECO requirements are also satisfied |
| Governance & PCR Hierarchy | Operator + Secretariat + Technical Committee (8–20 experts, PCR Review Panel); GPI → main PCR → c-PCR (stricter rule prevails; ≥8-week open consultation, 4-year default validity) | Board + Verification Body + independent Advisory Board SVR (8–11 unpaid experts authoring Part A & reviewing Part B; Part B valid 5 years, archived ≥10 years); external verifiers commissioned solely by IBU | Cross-operator mutual-recognition umbrella; CEN EN c-PCR standards prevail over programme-specific PCRs unless technically or legally justified |
| EPD Types & Pre-Verified Tools | 6 declaration types (single, multi-product, sector, trader, two prospective types) + Process Certification + 3 tiers of pre-verified tools (max 5-year validity) | Specific, average, representative, model (worst-case) + limited/extended individualisation; Verification Types A–D (Type C fully automatic tools sampled 3 EPDs/yr) | ECO EPDs for construction and related products; one digital dataset per product variant passing zero-error ILCD Validation Tool checks |
| Validity & Mandatory Update Trigger | Standard 5 years; annual follow-up; mandatory update or depublication within 6 months upon ≥10% indicator increase over included stages (excluding Module D; GWP-total for climate) | Standard 5 years; qualitative re-evaluation upon essential manufacturer-controlled changes; renewal without recalculation requires background data ≤10 years old | Follows EN 15804+A2 and member operator validity; 1-year transition to latest JRC EF 3.1 characterization factors |
| Electricity, Allocation & Mass Balance | Attributional default; market-based mandatory in A1–A3 (sub-national grids required for AU, BR, CA, CN, IN, US); 5% per-module cut-off; mass balance barred unless PCR-exempted | Horizontal calculation rules placed in SVR-authored PCR Part A; publication options across IBU.data, ÖKOBAUDAT, and InData | Mandatory cut-off [100:0]; bans ISO 22095 mass balance & Module D multi-recycling; programme-level choice of market- vs. location-based electricity; mandatory economic allocation (including below 1% revenue) for cement/concrete co-products |
| Mutual Recognition & Comparability Boundary | Explicitly states an MRA does not make EPDs comparable across programmes; five mandatory comparability gates; no ratings or direct comparisons inside an EPD | Supports ECO Platform mutual recognition; grouped EPDs must share function/class and identify covered products for building-level LCA | Mutual recognition of common quality floor; programme-level electricity choices remain; mandatory ECO Portal publication in ILCD+EPD v1.2 |

## Cross-Sector Divergences Across Construction, Batteries, and Agri-Food [#cross-sector-divergences]

<Prose ids="cl-epd31-pef-vs-en15804 cl-epd31-batteries-and-agrifood" />

Table 2. Cross-sector rule architecture across construction, electric-vehicle batteries, agri-food, and generic EPDs.

| Rule Dimension | Construction (EN 15804+A2 / ECO Platform) | Electric-Vehicle Batteries (Reg. (EU) 2023/1542 & JRC 2023 CFB-EV Draft) | Agri-Food / Dairy (EU Dairy PEFCR / (EU) 2021/2279) | Cross-Sector Generic EPD (International EPD System GPI v5.1.0) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legal Force & Drivers | Primarily voluntary declarations propelled by green-building schemes (DGNB/BNB), national decrees (FR/BE), and EU CPR/EPBD | Directly binding EU market-access regulation (declaration → performance classes → maximum thresholds); CFB-EV text is a JRC technical draft | Non-binding European Commission recommendation supporting harmonized category rules and market benchmarks | Voluntary international programme framework hosting sector PCRs across construction, industrial, and consumer goods |
| Reference Unit | Declared unit or functional unit (declared unit used when building-level function is unspecified) | Functional unit: 1 kWh total energy delivered over service life (light-duty EV default 160,000 km to 70%/65% SOCE) | Functional unit: 1 kg or 1 L consumer dairy product paired with a Representative Product benchmark | Functional unit where use-stage function is defined; declared unit for intermediates or multi-purpose products |
| System Boundary & Use Phase | Modular: minimum A1–A3, C, and D (separate B6 for permanently installed energy-using products; C/D exemptible under 3 criteria) | Mandatory 4 stages (raw materials, manufacturing, distribution, end of life); use phase explicitly excluded | Mandatory 5-stage cradle-to-grave including default household refrigeration and dishwashing | Cradle-to-grave default (A–C, separate Module D); cradle-to-gate allowed only when 4 criteria hold and product is not B2C |
| End-of-Life & Recycling Rule | Cut-off [100:0] + end-of-waste boundary + separate Module D beyond boundary (system expansion barred) | Mandatory Circular Footprint Formula (CFF: A = 0.2 metals, 0.5 plastics; R1 = 0 default; Table 2 default R2 up to 0.95) | Circular Footprint Formula (CFF) integrating recycled content, recycling, energy recovery, and disposal | Polluter-pays principle + 4 EN 15804 end-of-waste criteria + separate Module D (no system expansion or offsets) |
| Indicators & Weighting | 13 core + 6 additional EF midpoint indicators; normalization and weighting strictly prohibited | Single climate indicator: EF 3.1 GWP100 (g CO2eq/kWh); no normalization or weighting | 16 EF indicators with mandatory normalization, weighting into a single score, and performance classes | Multi-category indicators set by GPI/PCR; normalization, weighting, and superiority ratings barred inside EPDs |
| Primary Data & Verification | Allows average, representative, model EPDs, and pre-verified tools; independent third-party verifier review | Mandatory plant- and model-specific manufacturing data (DQR ≤ 1.5, no cross-plant sampling); notified-body audit with mandatory production-site visit | Splits company-specific and EF-compliant secondary data; IPCC Tier 2 methane floor (Tier 3/national allowed; same method mandatory for comparisons); independent verifier check | Primary data ≤5 yr, secondary ≤10 yr (proxies ≤10%); mandatory disclosure of A1–A3 GWP-GHG primary-data share |
| Comparability Scope | Comparable only at the building or assembly level, not as standalone product rankings | Statutory harmonized method and on-site notified-body audit directly support cross-model classes and thresholds | Cross-product comparison valid only under the same active PEFCR and Representative Product benchmark | Comparable only when all five gates hold (same PCR version, unit/performance, scope, LCIA version, and active validity) |

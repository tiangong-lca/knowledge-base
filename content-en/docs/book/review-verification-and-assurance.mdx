## Critical Review, Verification, Assurance, and Statutory Conformity Assessment [#four-oversight-regimes]

<Prose ids="cl-rev32-four-regimes cl-rev32-assurance-and-materiality" />

Table 1. Comparison of six public review, verification, assurance, and statutory conformity-assessment regimes across triggers, independence safeguards, assurance levels, site visits, and update thresholds.

| Regime Dimension | ILCD Review Schemes (2010) | EU PEF Recommendation (EU) 2021/2279 (Annex I §8 & Annex II §A.2.9) | GHG Protocol Product Standard (2011, Ch. 12) | International EPD System GPI v5.1.0 (2026-11-10) | IBU General Instructions v2.1 (2022) & ECO Platform V8.0 (2024) | EU Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 & JRC CFB-EV Draft (2023) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trigger & legal status | Voluntary public guidance covering 12 external LCA work cases | Non-binding EU Recommendation; mandatory verification & validation for any external PEF communication | Voluntary product GHG standard; mandatory assurance to claim compliance (no ISO 14044 comparative assertions) | Voluntary international EPD programme; mandatory third-party verification before publication | European construction EPD operator & mutual-recognition rules; mandatory third-party verification | Binding EU market-access law (Module A self-declaration barred for Art. 7–8); CFB-EV is a JRC technical draft |
| Review object & architecture | Level 1 independent external review or Level 2 panel (at least 3 external reviewers), plus stakeholder panel for high-stakes cases | Verification & validation of study, report, and communication vehicles; PEFCR panel of at least 3 legally separate members | First-party or third-party assurance via verification (e.g., ISO 14064-3) or critical review (expert or panel of at least 3) | Three routes: individual EPD verification, ISO/IEC 17065 process certification, 3 pre-verified tool classes; Technical Committee reviews PCRs | IBU Types A–D (individual, semi-auto tool, fully-auto random sampling, individualized model); ECO 16-domain core checklist | Notified body under Module D1 (series production QA) or Module G (non-series unit verification), with ID number beside CE mark |
| Independence & appointment | 1-year cooling-off on relevant ties for both person and employer; accreditation not mandated | External verifier; EN ISO/IEC 17020:2012 principles; 3-year consultancy disclosure; association payroll barred from PEFCR panel | First party relies on distinct internal reporting lines & disclosure; third party is separate entity (recommended for procurement/labels) | Certification bodies should be accredited to ISO/IEC 17029 and ISO 14065 (mandatory ISO/IEC 17065 for process certification); Technical Committee–approved individuals barred from outsourcing; same verifier allowed for tool & its EPDs | IBU allocates and exclusively pays verifier (5 working days conflict objection; mandatory rotation on tool renewal); ECO holds no common pool | Art. 25 bars design, manufacture, shareholding & consultancy ties and core delegation; CFB-EV §7.1 bars multi-body forum shopping |
| Assurance level & exclusions | Assurance Level 1 (single/team) or Level 2 (panel of 3 or more) | Reasonable assurance; splits method compliance (verification) from reliable, credible, correct data/communication (validation) | Limited (negative opinion) or reasonable (positive opinion); absolute assurance never provided | Reasonable assurance (high confidence, not absolute certainty) | Risk-based random plausibility checks; IBU §12.1 excludes factual truth beyond plausibility, software reliability & LCIA code; ECO leaves factual integrity to manufacturer | Statutory conformity approval; notified body inspects full model & files and reports refusals/withdrawals to authorities |
| Site visits & confidentiality | No site-visit mandate specified; balances stakeholder input with confidentiality | Mandatory production-site visit for company-specific data (PEFCR exempt); four-condition gate (omit inputs only, keep all outputs) | Risk-based; value-chain gaps allow downgrade to limited assurance, critical review, or supplier assurance reliance | Verifier chooses on-desk or on-site at equal reliability; confidential specifics disclosed in non-public English LCA report | IBU uses 6 desk/benchmark methods with no site-visit mandate; background report confidential to verifier | CFB-EV §7.3 mandates production-site visit for company-specific data; Module D1 adds periodic & unexpected visits; same 4 confidentiality rules |
| Validity & numeric triggers | No fixed percentage materiality threshold | Valid up to 3 years; significant mistakes above 5%; mandatory update if communicated category worsens above 10.0% or total score worsens above 5.0% | No fixed percentage threshold; distinguishes materiality (known misstatements) from statistical uncertainty | 5-year EPD validity & annual follow-up; indicator increase of 10% or more (excl. Module D) triggers 6-month update; major tool error above 10% | 5-year EPD validity; ECO V8.0 sets no percentage cutoff; IBU Type D allows a 5% margin over worst-case model product | Module D1 ongoing surveillance & change assessment; 10-year retention of technical, QA, and conformity files |

## Beyond Single Studies: Dataset Review, Pre-Verified Tools, and Process Certification [#datasets-tools-process-certification]

<Prose ids="cl-rev32-dataset-review cl-rev32-preverified-tools cl-rev32-process-certification" />

Table 2. Four oversight objects beyond single-study critical review: background LCI datasets, non-integrated pre-verified tools, integrated or fully pre-verified tools, and EPD process certification.

| Oversight Object | Upfront Qualification & Entry Gate | Pre-Publication External Check per Output | Anti-Manipulation & Integrity Safeguards | Periodic Audit & Quantitative Tolerance | Validity & Re-Verification Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background LCI dataset (UNEP/SETAC 2011 & JRC EF Guide v2.0) | Independent external review plus pre-entry validation recommended for public datasets; EF restricts to Type 1 (3 or more independent, 1 or more external) or Type 2 (2 independent, 1 or more external) meeting ILCD Entry Level (ILCD-EL) floors | Reviewed prior to library entry with signed review report attached as permanent metadata (additional ISO 14044 panel required if used in public comparative assertion) | Separates data gaps (n/a) from numeric zero; full unit-process files supplied behind aggregated datasets; reviewer certifies two-digit (X.X, one-decimal) DQR | EF mandates "Yes" on all Table 5 items, Look@LCI discrepancy of 1% or less, every DQR criterion at most 3.0, and cut-off at most 3.0% | Core content changes (Case A1: LCI, DQR, LCIA results, or process name) trigger full or partial re-review and a new UUID |
| Non-integrated pre-verified LCA/EPD tool (ECO V1.1 / GPI §8.6 / IBU Type B) | Mandatory upfront tool verification plus full verification of at least 1 real or fictive pilot EPD (ECO V1.1 excludes Model/worst-case EPD) | Yes: simplified pre-publication verification per EPD covering input/output plausibility, additional info, and formal items | Model locking against user edits; ECO V1.1 requires fixed-input integrity checks before every run on unlocked tools; tool name and version declared in EPD | Per-EPD simplified checks plus version consistency; GPI treats tool defects shifting results above 10% as major deviations | Maximum 5-year validity without changes (bounded by linked PCR expiry); numeric or formal changes trigger re-verification |
| Integrated or fully pre-verified EPD tool (ECO V1.1 Part 4 / GPI §8.6.9 / IBU Type C) | Upfront tool verification, pilot EPD full check, and QA management audit (IBU 9-item filing to independent Advisory Board [SVR]; GPI portal API integration) | No: individual EPDs may be published prior to annual third-party sample verification | Locked model plus dual tool-change and EPD-generation logbooks; ECO/IBU require four-eyes manual input checks (except declared configurators) or verified production/accounting links; GPI relies on automated feeds, user pre-verification, or automated plausibility checks | Annual logbook review plus EPD sample audit (IBU: 3 EPDs per product group if 5 or more/year; ECO exception for low volumes such as 3 or fewer; GPI: sample across owners, categories, and sites) | Maximum 5-year validity; IBU mandates 10-year archiving and mandatory verifier rotation on renewal; numeric or PCR changes trigger re-verification |
| EPD process certification (International EPD System GPI v5.1.0 §8.5) | Exclusively via ISO/IEC 17065 accredited certification bodies; requires documented PDCA workflow and internal verification | No external per-EPD check, but independent internal verifier must complete full Section 8.4 verification and report before publication | Internal assessors barred from checking own work, annual top-management review, and written process assurance statement | Annual certification-body audit verifying at least 1 EPD per product category and 1 alternating manufacturing site | Maximum 1-year certificate validity without changes; systemic non-conformity can trigger correction or withdrawal of published EPDs |

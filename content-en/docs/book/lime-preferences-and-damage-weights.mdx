Table 1. Comparison of monetary damage weights published in Table 7 of the two LIME1 articles (three selected safeguard subjects).

| Damage category | 2003 Japanese paper, Table 7 | 2004 English paper, Table 7 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human health | 572,000 JPY/DALY | 9,700,000 JPY/DALY |
| Primary production | 17.8 JPY/dry metric ton | 20,200 JPY/dry metric ton |
| Social assets | 4,460 JPY per 1,000 JPY loss | 10,000 JPY per 10,000 JPY loss |

Note: Recalculating the Japanese social-assets row from printed regression coefficients gives roughly 4,479.71 JPY per 1,000 JPY loss, whereas Table 7 lists 4,460 JPY. In the English paper, multiplying Table 3 by Table 7 yields an annual social-assets total of 2.29 × 10¹² JPY, whereas Table 8 prints 3.61 × 10¹² JPY. Thus the discrepancy appears in the unit valuation for the Japanese paper and in the annual monetized total for the English paper.

Table 2. Four representative weighting and valuation routes for cross-category trade-offs in life cycle assessment (LCA).

| Weighting and valuation route | Aggregation level and output scale | Source of valuation authority | Entry of normalization denominator | Rank-reversal triggers with unchanged inventory | Institutional boundary for public comparative assertions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Swiss ecological scarcity (UBP 2013/2021) | Inventory or midpoint converted directly to environmental impact points (UBP); single strictest pathway per substance | Domestic statutory environmental limits and political reduction targets | `(1/Fₙ) × (F/Fₖ)²`; simplifies to `F/Fₖ²` under a domestic single-source basis, retains external `Fₙ` across regions | Changes in statutory critical flows, base-year current flow updates, or switching between consumption and production perspectives | Constitutes value-based weighting; under ISO 14044 convention, not permitted for comparative assertions disclosed to the public |
| EU Environmental Footprint (EF 3.1; Sala et al. 2018) | 16 midpoint categories summed across pathways into a dimensionless single score (pt) | 50:50 public and expert importance multiplied by 0.17–0.87 scientific robustness factors | Each midpoint unit multiplied by `WFᵢ/NFᵢ`, with global per-capita annual reference `NFᵢ` inversely scaling the conversion rate | Switching outdoor versus indoor air sub-compartments, 0.17 toxicity robustness discount, or global `NFᵢ` version updates | Mandatory step in EU Recommendation for Product and Organisation Environmental Footprint (PEF/OEF); comparisons permitted only under matching Product Environmental Footprint Category Rules (PEFCR) with disaggregated profiles disclosed alongside |
| Cultural-perspective endpoint stop (ReCiPe 2016) | 17 midpoints aggregated to three areas of protection (`DALY`, `species·yr`, `USD2013`), then deliberately stopped | Natural-science exposure–effect models and three cultural perspectives (I/H/E); no cross-area single-score weights issued | Core method excludes cross-area normalization and weighting (software add-ons are not part of the original method) | 20-, 100-, and 1,000-year time horizons and climate-health inclusion thresholds (e.g., roughly 160.5-fold shift in CO₂ relative to PM₂.₅) | Reports three incommensurable endpoint scores separately; any cross-area weighting remains subject to the public comparative-assertion prohibition |
| Stated-preference endpoint monetization (Japan LIME1/LIME2) | Four safeguard-subject endpoint damages converted to a single Japanese yen scale (JPY) | Household marginal willingness to pay (WTP) elicited via conjoint analysis of hypothetical policy cards | Direct unit sum `ΣDₑvₑ` bypasses external normalization; annual share weights `Nₑvₑ/ΣNⱼvⱼ` reintroduce national annual reference `Nₑ` | Survey baseline damage magnitudes, switching between tax and social-asset numeraires, or changing between 2003, 2004, and 2012 survey tables | Constitutes social-preference monetary weighting; subject to ISO public comparative-assertion prohibition, with direct applicability limited to the sampled Japanese population |

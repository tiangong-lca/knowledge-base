Table 1. Comparison of category architecture, reference basis, and endpoint boundaries across the two major chemical-toxicity framework families.

| Framework and Underlying Model | Midpoint Categories and Units | Terrestrial and Marine Ecotoxicity Coverage | Endpoint Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| ReCiPe 2016 (USES-LCA 2.0 + USEtox substance database) | 5: cancer and non-cancer (urban-air `1,4-DCB eq.`); freshwater, marine, and terrestrial ecotoxicity (compartment-specific `1,4-DCB eq.`) | Includes marine and industrial soil; excludes agricultural soil to avoid double counting with land use | Multiplies by perspective-invariant `1,4-DCB` damage factors to convert to `DALY` and `species·yr` |

Table 2. Examples of freshwater metal ecotoxicity reordering between EF 3.0 and EF 3.1 (`CTUe/kg`).

| Metal Emitted to Freshwater | EF 3.0 (Legacy USEtox `0.5/HC50`) | EF 3.1 (`0.2/HC20` + EU Toxicological Dossiers) | Magnitude and Mechanism of Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trivalent antimony (`Sb³⁺`) | `1.22×10²` | `1.43×10²` | +18% (no EU dossier; extrapolated solely via `HC20 = 0.34 × HC50`) |
| Aluminum (`Al`) | `4.09×10⁵` | `4.35×10³` | −99% (benchmark switch combined with EU dossier replacement) |
| Copper (`Cu`) | `9.92×10⁴` | `4.65×10¹` | Printed as −100% in source table (drop of over three orders of magnitude) |
| Cadmium (`Cd`) | `2.29×10⁵` | `6.74×10⁵` | +195% (rises to highest-ranked metal) |
| Silver (`Ag`) | `1.94×10⁴` | `1.77×10⁵` | +813% (moves into top tier) |
| Mercury (`Hg`) | `2.21×10³` | `3.32×10⁴` | +1,401% (relative weight against copper flips by ~32,000×) |

Table 1. Accounting basis for energy carriers and natural uranium across two resource midpoint systems (ReCiPe 2016 fossil fuel potential and EF 3.1 fossil resource use).

| Input | ecoinvent higher heating value (MJ-eq) | ReCiPe 2016 midpoint | EF 3.1 Resource use, fossils |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crude oil | 45.8 /kg | 1 kg oil-eq/kg | 1 MJ/MJ (net calorific value) |
| Natural gas | 38.3 /Nm³ | 0.84 kg oil-eq/Nm³ | 1 MJ/MJ (net calorific value) |
| Hard coal | 19.1 /kg | 0.42 kg oil-eq/kg (unrounded ≈ 0.4170) | 1 MJ/MJ (net calorific value) |
| Brown coal | 9.9 /kg | 0.22 kg oil-eq/kg | 1 MJ/MJ (net calorific value) |
| Peat | 9.9 /kg | 0.22 kg oil-eq/kg | 1 MJ/MJ (net calorific value) |
| Natural uranium | — | Not in fossil table; mineral SOP 35.8 (economic reserves) and 25.2 (ultimate recoverable resources) kg Cu-eq/kg | 1 MJ/MJ, included under Resource use, fossils |

Note: ReCiPe 2016 fossil midpoint factors are obtained by rounding the ratio of each fuel's higher heating value to that of crude oil; the Van Oers et al. (2020) supplementary workbook keeps natural uranium under elemental depletion, with a cumulative ADP of about 0.007823 kg Sb-eq/kg (rounded to 7.8 × 10⁻³ in the main paper table).

Table 1. Comparison of water-resource assessment architectures across four major life cycle impact assessment (LCIA) frameworks.

| Framework | Midpoint Indicator and Independent Variable | Endpoint Damage Pathways | Spatial and Methodological Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ILCD 2011 | Swiss ecological scarcity method (6 scarcity classes, Level III) | 0 (not evaluated) | Rejects pure withdrawal summation (MEEuP) and scarcity-free exergy extraction (CEENE) |
| EF 3.1 | AWARE proxy midpoint (`m³ world-eq.`, capped at 0.1–100, Level III) | 0 | Defaults to national annual unspecified-use factors; nets 7 positive and 4 negative same-country flows |
| ReCiPe 2016 | Water Consumption Potential, WCP (uniform `1 m³/m³`, no scarcity weight) | 3: malnutrition health, terrestrial plants, estuarine freshwater fish | Defers all scarcity and vulnerability to endpoints; industrial groundwater does not deduct surface returns |
| LC-IMPACT | 0 midpoints | 2: malnutrition health across 11,050 watersheds; global wetland species extinction at 0.05° | Argues partial midpoints break rank consistency with endpoints; quantifies damage directly |

## Capital Goods, Cut-off Rules, and Temporal Baselines [#capital-goods-cut-off-rules-and-temporal-baselines]

<Prose ids="cl-db27-capital-goods-asymmetry cl-db27-cutoffs-and-temporal-lag" />

Table 1. Foundational methodological comparison of five major background database paradigms across system models, aggregation depth, geography and market modeling, capital goods, and cut-off conventions.

| Methodological Axis | ecoinvent v3 | Sphera MLC (formerly GaBi) | EU EF 3.0 / 3.1 Secondary Data | National Public Libraries (USLCI / IDEA / CLCD) | EEIO / MRIO (EXIOBASE 3 / USEEIO) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multifunctionality & recycling | Raw datasets decoupled from linking: Cut-off, Cut-off EN 15804, APOS, and Consequential | Centrally governed engineering allocation or system expansion by sector | Mandatory Circular Footprint Formula (CFF) partitioning primary and recycled burdens | Typically fixed to a single attributional convention per library/sector | National supply-use tables projected via industry- or commodity-technology assumptions |
| Delivery granularity & transparency | Transparent unit processes (UPR) enabling disassembly and Monte Carlo propagation | Largely vertically/horizontally aggregated LCI (confidentiality) plus selected UPRs | Primarily ILCD-EF partly terminated systems; minor subprocesses non-reusable standalone | USLCI open UPRs; IDEA hybrid process-IO; CLCD commercial background library | Macro sector-level monetary-environmental matrices; no plant-level disassembly |
| Geography & residual rules | Strict non-overlapping geographies; sub-national grids for large countries; dynamic GLO/RoW residuals | Country and regional production/consumption averages maintained by one team | EU-27+3 and major trade partners bound to official location codes | Anchored in domestic electricity, fuel, and industrial statistics; imports often link globally | National/regional economic boundaries; USEEIO v2.0 separates domestic vs. foreign impacts |
| Transport & electricity boundaries | Transforming activities (gate) separated from market activities (embedded freight, trade, losses) | Most industrial profiles delivered at factory gate; foreground often adds transport explicitly | Combines residual electricity mixes (net of tracked renewables) and explicit transport rules | Voltage levels, grid losses, and embedded transport vary by library and require verification | Transport and electricity closed as economic sectors via purchaser-price and margin matrices |
| Capital goods & cut-off rules | Capital goods and infrastructure included by default across most processes; process cut-offs | Industrial profiles frequently exclude or selectively include capital goods | Cumulative cut-offs < 3.0% for mass, energy, and each impact category; rule-bound capital goods | Uneven capital-goods coverage; standalone USLCI historically has unclosed upstream gaps | Zero upstream economy-wide truncation (capital formation depends on endogenization) |

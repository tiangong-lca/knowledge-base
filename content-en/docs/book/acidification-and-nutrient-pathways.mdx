Table 1. Eutrophication category architecture and coverage across four mainstream impact assessment frameworks.

| Framework | Terrestrial nutrient enrichment | Freshwater eutrophication | Marine eutrophication | Formally recommended endpoints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ILCD 2011 / EF 3.1 | Accumulated Exceedance (mol N eq; EF weight 3.71%) | EUTREND (kg P eq; EF weight 2.80%) | EUTREND (kg N eq; EF weight 2.96%) | 0 (ILCD lists an interim freshwater option only) |
| UNEP 2019 Global Guidance | Not listed separately | Phosphorus transport and species occurrence (kg P eq) | Nitrogen transport and bottom-water hypoxia (kg N eq) | 2 local reversible endpoints (PDF·m³·yr) |
| ReCiPe 2016 | Folded into terrestrial acidification (not separate) | 0.5° grid phosphorus model (kg P eq) | Not included (0 categories) | 1 freshwater endpoint (6.1×10⁻⁷ species·yr/kg P eq, constant across all three perspectives) |
| LC-IMPACT | Not listed separately | No midpoint; direct endpoint | No midpoint; direct endpoint | 2 global extinction-risk endpoints (PDF·yr; freshwater covers fish only) |

Each of the four external electricity datasets below measures a distinct quantity; using any of them to estimate emissions still requires verifying the region, time period, and delivery point.

| Dataset | Question Addressed Here |
| --- | --- |
| eGRID2023 Revision 2 | Annual average direct CO₂ emission rate of regional power plants, with annual net generation as the denominator. |
| EIA-930 (2023) | Hourly average power (MW) for generation, demand, and interregional interchange, multiplied by one hour to obtain energy (MWh), stamped at hour-ending. |
| AVERT 4.3.0 | Modeled operational response of existing generating units in the 2023 Texas (TE) region when end-use electricity demand changes. |
| Cambium 2024 | Modeled long-run response when a demand perturbation allows both operation and generating capacity to adjust; the example here uses the 2025 simulation year. |

Even when expressed per unit of the same electricity metric, annual emission attribution, hourly energy balance, unit-level operational response, and long-run scenarios answer different questions. Comparing emission rates across these datasets also requires checking whether each denominator reflects electricity generated at power plants or electricity delivered to end users.

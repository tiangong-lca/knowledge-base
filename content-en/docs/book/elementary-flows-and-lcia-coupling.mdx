## Collapse, Double Counting, and Silent Zero Characterization [#collapse-double-counting-and-silent-zero-characterization]

<Prose ids="cl-db28-many-to-one-and-one-to-many cl-db28-aggregate-flows-and-double-counting cl-db28-stoichiometry-unit-sign-traps cl-db28-silent-unmapped-zero-failure cl-db28-software-handling-and-co-validation" />

Table 1. Five structural failure modes in cross-database elementary-flow mapping, high-frequency trigger flows, LCIA consequences, and engineering audit defenses.

| Failure Mode | Underlying Mechanism | High-Frequency Trigger Flows | Consequence for LCIA Results | Engineering Audit Defense |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LCIA accounting-logic clash | Incompatible water-netting or biogenic-carbon sign conventions between source and target LCIA | EF 3.1 7-positive / 4-negative water flows; fossil/biogenic/dLUC carbon (0/0 vs. −1/+1) | Non-consumptive cooling water counted as consumption (order-of-magnitude inflation); spurious negative carbon | Audit water exchange direction and return netting; enforce uniform biogenic-carbon accounting |
| Many-to-one collapse / one-to-many fabricated precision | Non-bijective match between fine sub-compartments (urban/rural/stack height) and unspecified | PM2.5, PM2.5–10, PM10; Cr(III)/Cr(VI); indoor vs. outdoor air | Permanent loss of exposure detail, or multiplied health/toxicity distortion from wrong proxies | Preserve source sub-compartment metadata; disclose proxy choices and run sensitivity checks |
| Aggregate group-flow overlap | Speciated compounds and aggregate group totals coexist in one process; row-level mapping lacks balance context | NMVOC, VOC, total PAHs, unspecified pesticides | Double-counting or omission in photochemical ozone and toxicity categories | Screen processes for coexisting speciated and group flows; verify net-of-speciated subtraction |
| Reference-substance, unit & sign traps | Same element expressed as different chemical species, heating-value/dry-wet bases, or sign conventions | Elemental P vs. P₂O₅ (0.436); N vs. NO₃⁻; LHV vs. HHV; Nm³ vs. kg | Missing stoichiometric or energy multipliers cause >2× systematic bias or sign inversion | Require explicit FlowPropertyFactor and conversion factors in flow maps; ban name-only swaps |
| Silent unmapped drop-to-zero | Solver assigns zero impact without error when flow UUID, spelling, or context fails to match | Cross-imported methane, heavy metals, pesticides, regionalized water flows | Disguises mapping failure as zero environmental burden, severely underestimating impacts | Co-validate LCIA methods with native flow lists; run zero-characterization audits on dominant mass and impact flows |

Table 2. Five structural mismatch dimensions in cross-database technosphere process mapping, model distortions, and automation limits.

| Mismatch Dimension | Apparent Surface Similarity | Resulting Model Distortion | Automatable from Text or Codes? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Non-bijective classifications | CPC/ISIC cross-walked to BEA/NAICS or HS codes | Sector aggregation mismatch; physical processes mixed with monetary sectors | No; requires explicit aggregation weights or hybrid double-counting rules |
| Product & technical grade | Identical material name with differing purity, moisture, or recycled content | Systematic shift in upstream energy and emission intensity per unit mass | No; must verify grade, dry/wet basis, and flow properties |
| Technology route split | One product split into multiple production routes across releases or libraries | Forced 1:1 mapping masks technology structure and marginal differences | Only conditionally via declarative disaggregation tables with explicit shares |
| Market vs. transforming role | Market mix (with transport/trade) swapped with gate-to-gate production | Silent double-counting or omission of transport and trade losses | No; must be reconciled against foreground transport boundaries |
| System model & carbon rules | Cut-off mixed with Consequential, or −1/+1 mixed with 0/0 biogenic carbon | Double-counted/omitted recycling burdens; broken carbon mass balance | Prohibited silently; requires unified system rules or explicit disclosure |

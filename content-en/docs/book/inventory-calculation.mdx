## Result Decomposition, Perturbation, and Key Issues [#result-decomposition-perturbation-and-key-issues]

<Prose ids="cl-dec36-contribution-and-perturbation cl-wallext52-matrix-decomposition cl-dec36-ecoinvent96-perturbation-and-key-issue cl-dec36-ilcd-priority-and-bridge" />

Table 1. Comparison of five post-solution numerical interpretation and diagnostic pathways (synthesized from Heijungs & Kleijn, 2001; Heijungs, Suh & Kleijn, 2005; ILCD General Guide, 2010).

| Diagnostic method | Extra inputs required | Core question answered | Empirical properties and key limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contribution analysis | None | Which processes, stages, or flows carry the largest direct shares of total output | Under 1 second on ecoinvent'96; blind to false negatives, pie charts fail under negative shares, restricted to direct attribution on a completed system |
| Analytical perturbation analysis | None (matrix differentiation) | Which intermediate economic flows or coefficients amplify output most sharply | Under 5 minutes for all 571 ecoinvent'96 flows (~300 hours numerically); multipliers sum to zero, magnitudes above 1 arise from recursive loops, valid locally only |
| Key-issue analysis (variance decomposition) | Input standard deviations / variances | Which uncertain inputs explain the largest share of total output variance | Strictly non-negative shares avoiding negative-contribution ambiguity; 3 input coefficients drive 69% of ecoinvent'96 carbon dioxide variance (two in P293 account for 60%); diverges from Monte Carlo on near-zero or sign-flipping flows |
| Paired discernibility analysis | Input probability distributions (paired draws) | Accounting for shared-background co-variation, how often does one option outperform another | Resolves overlapping individual 95% confidence intervals (Portugal vs. Italy 97/100); no analytical shortcut; tracks rank frequency rather than magnitude |
| ILCD discrete scenarios & Priority Matrix | Alternative modeling choices + data quality ratings | Which methodological choices alter outcomes, and which high-sensitivity, low-quality inputs merit priority refinement | Targets allocation, cut-off, and impact-method choice uncertainty; cannot automatically detect omissions (ignorance), which still require peer review |

**Table: Phase Organization, Comparison Gates, Weighting Rules, and Review Governance Across Four Operational LCA Guides**

| Comparison Dimension | CML 2001/2002 (Part 2a: Guide) | ILCD 2010 (Detailed Guidance) | EU PEF 2021 ((EU) 2021/2279, Annex I) | GHG Protocol Product Standard (2011) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase and Workflow Organization | 4 phases (goal and scope, inventory, impact assessment, interpretation) plus Chapter 1 project procedure management (5 chapters total); tiered into simplified, detailed baseline, and optional extensions | 4 phases structured as multi-round iterative refinement loops driven by completeness, sensitivity, and consistency checks | Standardized PEF/PEFCR workflow; reports full life cycle results both with and without the use stage | Business goals → principles → scope → boundary → data quality → allocation → uncertainty → results → assurance → reporting and reduction targets |
| Decision Context and Comparative-Assertion Gate | Detailed level aligns with ISO; public comparative assertions are restricted and no mandatory category-wide rules are included | Routes studies upfront by decision context: Situation A (micro-level), Situation B (meso/macro-level), and Situation C1/C2 (descriptive accounting) | Distinguishes general comparisons from comparative assertions; both are permitted only when following a valid PEFCR | Positioned for longitudinal emissions tracking of a single product; explicitly does not support ISO 14044 comparative assertions (cross-product comparisons require separate product rules) |
| Impact Categories and Weighting Rules | Midpoint problem-oriented multi-category characterization; normalization strongly recommended; grouping and weighting optional with no baseline method; paraphrases ISO 14042 prohibition on weighting in public comparative assertions | Multi-category impact assessment; matches characterization and optional normalization/weighting to study goal and decision context | Mandates all 16 Environmental Footprint (EF) impact categories with no exclusions; mandatory normalization and weighting after characterization, with optional aggregation into a single score | Accounts solely for greenhouse gases (GHG) as a single impact category; involves no cross-category trade-offs or weighting |
| Interpretation Checks and Review/Assurance Governance | Chapter 5 performs consistency, completeness, and sensitivity/uncertainty checks, reporting result bandwidths and limitations | Chapter 11 requires scoping the review type to check consistency and validity; sets ISO 14040/14044 as the baseline pending dedicated review rules | Section 8.1 mandates independent verification and validation for any form of external communication | Section 12.2 defines a three-party structure and requires first-party or third-party assurance of the product carbon inventory |

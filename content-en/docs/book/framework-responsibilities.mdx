**Table: Whole-Book Analytical Map — How Four Operational LCA Guides Divide Phase Responsibilities and Where Each Topic Is Developed**

| Analytical task | Core question | Key divergences across CML 2001 / ILCD 2010 / EU PEF 2021 / GHG Protocol 2011 | Where developed in this book |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal, scope, and functional reference | Which decision is supported, how is service equivalence fixed, and what sits in the foreground or cut-off? | ILCD splits Situation A/B/C1/C2; PEF mandates 16 categories and names the verifier at Goal; GHG Protocol targets single-product tracking; CML defines three sophistication tiers | Part II: [Goal and Scope Definition](/en/docs/book/goal-and-scope/), [Functional Reference](/en/docs/book/service-and-reference/) |
| Inventory modeling and multifunctionality | How is the process network closed, and how are co-products and recycling partitioned or credited? | Cut-off and hybrid IO rules diverge; attributional partitioning, consequential marginal supply, electricity scales, and the Circular Footprint Formula (CFF) apply under distinct conditions | Part II: [Process Networks](/en/docs/book/inventory-calculation/)–[Hybrid Inventory Analysis](/en/docs/book/inventory-boundary-and-hybrid/); Part III: [Joint Production](/en/docs/book/joint-production/)–[Material Recycling Chains](/en/docs/book/recycling-chains-and-accounting/) |
| Impact assessment and cross-category trade-offs | How do inventory flows become midpoint/endpoint indicators, and when are normalization and weighting allowed? | CML strongly recommends normalization and notes the ban on weighting in public comparative assertions; PEF mandates normalization and weighting across all 16 categories; GHG Protocol covers climate change only | Part IV: [The Structure of Impact Assessment](/en/docs/book/midpoint-endpoint-value-choices/) and pathway chapters; Part V: [Ecological Scarcity Weighting](/en/docs/book/ecological-scarcity-and-targets/)–[Life Cycle Costing and Integrated Assessment](/en/docs/book/cost-perspectives-and-sustainability-combination/) |
| Data infrastructure, rules, and assurance | Where do background data come from, how are quality and uncertainty calibrated, and how are results verified and translated into policy? | ILCD takes ISO as its review floor; PEF mandates independent verification for any external communication and limits comparisons to PEFCRs; GHG Protocol uses first- or third-party assurance | Part VI: [Background Database Evolution and Methodological Divergences](/en/docs/book/lca-database-landscape-and-history/)–[Data Quality Scores and Probability](/en/docs/book/data-quality-and-probability/); Part VII: [Product Footprint Rules](/en/docs/book/product-footprint-rules-and-practice/)–[Life Cycle Results in Policy](/en/docs/book/lca-results-in-policy/); Part VIII: [Wall Service Comparison](/en/docs/book/wall-service-to-comparison/)–[Revisable Judgment](/en/docs/book/revisable-judgment/) |

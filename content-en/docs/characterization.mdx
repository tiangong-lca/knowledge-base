---
title: A Missing Characterization Factor Is Not Zero
description: Distinguish characterization records, coverage status, and numerical totals so that essential diagnostic information is preserved for interpreting impact results.
---

When inspecting a flow's characterized impact score in life cycle impact assessment (LCIA), separate three things: the inventory quantity, the flow-mapping record, and the basis for the characterization factor. A zero entry can still conceal unresolved diagnostic information that an aggregated total alone cannot explain.

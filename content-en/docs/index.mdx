---
title: About the English Edition
description: Meaning-equivalent English edition of The Evolution and Foundations of Life Cycle Assessment (in preparation).
---

The English edition of *The Evolution and Foundations of Life Cycle Assessment* is being authored directly at the meaning level from primary standards, handbooks, and peer-reviewed studies, sharing the same evidence, calculation inputs, and revision governance as the Chinese edition.

Chapters are published progressively by part. While the English chapters are in preparation, the complete 32-chapter Chinese edition is available under the Chinese view.

## How to Read This Lineage and Verification Boundaries [#reading-and-verification]

Life cycle assessment (LCA) terminology, accounting rules, and data formats have evolved over more than half a century. Version upgrades routinely shift system boundaries, recycling allocation, heating-value conventions, normalization denominators, or verification gates, making results from different editions non-interchangeable. This appendix complements the [LCA Development Timeline](/en/docs/appendices/lca-timeline/): whereas the timeline weaves cross-domain events along one chronology, these four lineage tables isolate each family's version progression, structural shifts relative to predecessors, and source-inspection boundaries.

To prevent extrapolation beyond inspected evidence, every row explicitly records access status. Public primary reports, peer-reviewed papers, open specifications, and Official Journal texts were inspected directly. Copyrighted or paywalled full texts of International Organization for Standardization (ISO, including Technical Specifications [ISO/TS]), European Standards (EN, including Amendments [+A1/+A2] and Corrigenda [+AC]), and Chinese recommended national standards (GB/T, where `/T` denotes recommended status)—covering ISO 14040/14044 clauses beyond the public preview and later amendments, ISO 14067, ISO 14025, ISO 21930, ISO 14071, ISO/TS 14072, EN 15804+A2, and GB/T 24067-2024—as well as tiered-license commercial database internals were not directly inspected; their structural milestones rely strictly on public guides, official metadata, and open peer-reviewed literature without reconstructing uninspected clauses.

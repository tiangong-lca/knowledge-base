## Overview of Ten Major Impact Assessment Methods and Models [#method-families-overview]

Evaluating the exact same life cycle inventory (LCI) under different life cycle impact assessment (LCIA) methods frequently alters hotspot rankings and comparative conclusions. Divergences stem not only from midpoint versus endpoint cutpoints, but also from spatial granularity, integration time horizons, value framing, normalization and weighting stances, and boundary classification of flows such as natural uranium. This appendix consolidates the ten major frameworks and specialized models audited across Parts IV and V into three lookup layers: a method-architecture overview, category-by-category comparisons across thirteen mainstream impact categories, and an inventory of eight environmental blind spots still lacking mainstream consensus characterization.

Verification boundary note: The Institute of Environmental Sciences at Leiden University (CML) 2001/2002 guides, ReCiPe 2016, European Union (EU) Environmental Footprint (EF) 3.1, LC-IMPACT v1.0, IMPACT World+, United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) Global Guidance for LCIA (GLAM) v1.1, Swiss Ecological Scarcity 2013/2021, Japan's Life-cycle Impact assessment Method based on Endpoint modeling (LIME1/2), the chemical toxicity consensus model USEtox 1.0/2.0, and the Available WAter REmaining (AWARE) 1.x/2.0 models were verified directly from open primary reports, peer-reviewed papers, or official factor packages; LIME3 and USEtox 2.1 are traced via inspected public reports (uninspected versions such as USEtox 2.12 or copyrighted standards are not extrapolated). Where a cell notes that a category is not expanded or absent, that reflects either the verified absence of the category in that method or the bounded scope of inspected open documentation.

## Eight Consensus Blind Spots in Mainstream Impact Assessment [#consensus-blind-spots]

Every multi-indicator profile measures only the pathways represented in its characterization factor tables; unmodeled environmental pressures register as zero in single scores even when physical damage is substantial. The table below consolidates eight consensus blind spots verified in this book for use during scoping, critical review, and limitation disclosure.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.

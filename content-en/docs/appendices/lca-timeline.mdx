## Historical Arc and Verification Status [#historical-arc]

This appendix traces 27 key milestones in life cycle assessment (LCA) from 1969 to 2026 across five historical periods (early resource ledgers, framework formation and divergence, standardization and model deepening, guidance systems and regionalization, and regulatory mandates with prospective dynamics) and four methodological tracks (frameworks and standards, inventories and databases, impact assessment methods, and landmark case studies and policy).

Every milestone carries an explicit primary-source verification status: the 22 entries marked as verified against primary sources correspond directly to original reports, methodological papers, or official regulations inspected in this knowledge base, with clickable source tags linking to [Sources & Evidence](/en/sources/); the 5 early or paywalled standard milestones whose full original texts have not yet been inspected directly are transparently marked **Unverified** together with their current verification boundaries. A standalone view is also available at the [Development Timeline](/en/timeline/).

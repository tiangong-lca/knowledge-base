## Historical Arc and Verification Status [#historical-arc]

This appendix traces key milestones in life cycle assessment (LCA) from 1969 to 2026 across five historical periods (early resource ledgers, framework formation and divergence, standardization and model deepening, guidance systems and regionalization, and regulatory mandates with prospective dynamics) and four methodological tracks (frameworks and standards, inventories and databases, impact assessment methods, and landmark case studies and policy).

Entries grounded in inspected original reports, peer-reviewed papers, or official regulations link directly to [References](/en/sources/); early works and copyrighted standards whose full originals are not openly accessible are transparently marked as drawing on secondary accounts together with their evidentiary boundaries. A standalone view is also available at the [Timeline](/en/timeline/).

## How to Read This Matrix and Scope of Coverage [#how-to-read]

Chapters throughout this book examine individual rules across standards, operational guides, background databases, open-source software, and data schemas. This appendix synthesizes nine widely used frameworks, databases, and tools across nine recurring core concepts in three continuous cross-reference tables so readers—whether modeling, reviewing, teaching, or navigating regulation—can see at a glance what each concept means across systems, where rules genuinely diverge, and which chapters hold the primary-source analysis and worked evidence.

The nine rows span three institutional tiers. First come international baseline standards and operational guides: the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 14040/14044 series and related product/organizational standards (including China's recommended national standard GB/T 24067-2024, where `/T` denotes voluntary/recommended status), the Institute of Environmental Sciences at Leiden University (CML) guides and CML-IA impact assessment factor database, and the International Reference Life Cycle Data System (ILCD) Handbook. Second come market-disclosure and sector declaration regimes: the European Union (EU) Product Environmental Footprint (PEF), Organizational Environmental Footprint (OEF), and Environmental Footprint (EF) 3.1 method, the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol Product and Scope 3 standards, and European construction standard EN 15804+A2 (Amendment 2) together with Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) programme operators—the International EPD System General Programme Instructions (GPI v5.1.0), Germany's Institut Bauen und Umwelt (IBU, Institute for Building and Environment, v2.1), and the European construction EPD umbrella body ECO Platform. Third comes technical infrastructure for calculation and exchange: the ecoinvent background life cycle inventory (LCI) database, open-source modeling tools and schemas—openLCA (with its `olca-schema` exchange schema) and Brightway (with its declarative `randonneur` migration package)—and the open TianGong Life Cycle Data Specification (TIDAS, `tidas-spec` v0.3.0).

When reading the infrastructure tier (rows 7–9: ecoinvent, openLCA / Brightway, and TianGong TIDAS), distinguish "not prescribed at this layer" from a methodological gap. Background databases, solvers, and open data contracts deliberately separate data representation, matrix arithmetic, and schema checking from normative study rules: they do not choose a study's functional unit, normalization denominator, or public comparative-assertion eligibility, yet their choices on system linking, elementary-flow mapping, zero-factor handling, and review-status metadata directly govern whether results remain comparable. Superscripts <sup>[1]</sup> through <sup>[4]</sup> refer to the verification and paywalled-standard boundaries consolidated in the closing section.

## Verification Boundaries and Uninspected Copyrighted Standards [#verification-boundaries]

To preserve uniform evidentiary transparency across the book, every matrix entry is synthesized strictly from openly accessible primary reports, official gazettes, open-source documentation, and peer-reviewed papers verified in the linked chapters, without extrapolating or reconstructing uninspected paywalled clauses. Four cross-cutting boundaries apply:

- **[1] ISO series and Chinese national adoptions**: Directly inspected ISO primary texts are limited to the public previews of ISO 14040:2006 and ISO 14044:2006 (Foreword, Introduction, Scope, and Sections 4.1–4.2.3.5 through printed page 9) plus baseline definitions of elementary flows, waste, and cut-off verified in the linked chapters. See [Standardization and Institutionalization of LCA](/en/docs/book/standardization-and-institutionalization/); [Goal and Scope Definition](/en/docs/book/goal-and-scope/); [Product Footprint Rules](/en/docs/book/product-footprint-rules-and-practice/); [Review, Verification, and Assurance](/en/docs/book/review-verification-and-assurance/); [Life Cycle Results in Policy](/en/docs/book/lca-results-in-policy/).
- **[2] GHG Protocol companion boundary**: Both 2011 main standards (the Product Life Cycle Accounting and Reporting Standard and the Corporate Value Chain [Scope 3] Standard) were inspected page by page; the separate *Technical Guidance for Calculating Scope 3 Emissions* was not directly inspected. See [Product Footprint Rules](/en/docs/book/product-footprint-rules-and-practice/).
- **[3] European construction and EPD standards**: Full copyrighted texts of EN 15804+A2, EN 15978, EN 15941, European Committee for Standardization (CEN) complementary product category rules (EN c-PCRs), ISO/TS 14027/14029, and ISO 22057 were not directly inspected; all construction EPD entries derive strictly from publicly accessible International EPD System General Programme Instructions (GPI v5.1.0, 2026), IBU General Instructions (v2.1, 2022), ECO Platform guidelines (2024), and open peer-reviewed studies (Passer et al. 2015; Durão et al. 2020; Konradsen et al. 2024; Otero et al. 2026). See [Environmental Product Declarations and Sector Rules](/en/docs/book/epds-and-sector-rules/); [Review, Verification, and Assurance](/en/docs/book/review-verification-and-assurance/).
- **[4] Licensed commercial databases and proprietary software**: ecoinvent methodology, data-quality guidelines, and system-model architecture are verified from public guidelines and peer-reviewed papers, whereas full machine-readable commercial release packages (such as v3.11 in EcoSpold2 XML exchange format) are tier-licensed and their internal record tables were not inspected without a license; the same boundary applies to proprietary commercial software internals. Statutory compliance filings and third-party certifications must always rely on current licensed standards and authorized data packages. See [Background Database Evolution and Methodological Divergences](/en/docs/book/lca-database-landscape-and-history/); [Data Formats, Mapping, and Conversion Disclosure](/en/docs/book/exchange-formats-and-interoperability-governance/).

# Editorial and living review protocol

## Market-response source and argument checks

The market-response unit reconstructs the original 1999 procedure, its contemporary critique and concession, and the explicit 2004 procedural lineage without inferring influence from chronology. PSI average-mix arithmetic and its dissenting method positions are distinct from the synthetic response example. Reviews check exclusive direct-process conditions, actual supply versus capacity availability, equal extra service across response scenarios, loss-inclusive denominators and material-response stipulations. Source joins, positive activities, reader meaning and rendered publication receive separate checks. Restricted originals and actual query/exclusion logs remain outside reader pages; gaps never become source facts.

## Historical pilot scope and evidence frame

Publication starts with four bounded topics: flow/exchange/waste conventions; functional unit/reference flow in ILCD Format 1.1; ecoinvent system model overview; characterization coverage in openLCA. The history chapter is a research agenda with two source-bound software/database version nodes. The initial source frame is eight public official HTML documents, accessed 2026-11-03, selected for explanatory pilot value rather than a systematic literature search. No global recall or completeness estimate is claimed.

That initial source set was not a systematic review. All eight URLs were inspected at the cited sections. Current web pages are mutable; this first set does not bundle source HTML or claim immutable preservation. Current manuals were not independently checked against historical executables. ISO full text, national standards, PCR/EPD rules, historical original reports and native software execution are outside the completed review scope. A blocked EUR-Lex page and a missing ILCD mirror enumeration page provided no evidence; the accessible JRC ILCD ProcessDataSet documentation supplies the cited quantitative-reference fields.

## Claim method

A source statement paraphrases only the cited content. Interpretation explains a source within its scope. Synthesis connects explicit premises, keeps their caveats and cannot create new normative authority. Calculation records assumptions and arithmetic; hypothetical values remain hypothetical. Proposals identify rationale and alternatives rather than pretending a source required the proposal. Reviewed means agent source checking for the stated scope; it is not human expert approval or certification.

Every substantive factual MDX assertion renders its canonical catalog claim or is visibly marked as a hypothetical illustration, question, editorial policy or research task. Repeated explanatory prose must preserve the canonical statement's scope. Claim IDs are stable; meaning-changing edits create review obligations. Evidence links say supports, qualifies, contradicts or context. Counterevidence is retained rather than averaged into a confidence score. Absence from the bounded search is not evidence of nonexistence.

Concept labels are distinct from meaning. Each concept links definition claims scoped to their source systems; mappings require direction, conditions and supporting claims. The initial functional-unit/reference-flow relation records relatedness, not equivalence or universal computable conversion. Contextual mappings do not authorize transitive equivalence.

## Ongoing retrieval and updates

A maintainer checks official source announcements weekly, screens candidate literature monthly and plans quarterly topic synthesis, adjusting cadence through a recorded amendment when workload or change rates justify it. This is an initial maintenance policy, not evidence that any automated monitor is already running. Before expanding a topic, write its databases, multilingual queries, date limits, eligibility rules, screening responsibility, full-text access needs and stop conditions. Preserve exclusions and failed access.

On new evidence, capture source identity and rights, extract exact locators, assess support/contradiction and identify affected claims. Changes to source content, claim premises, concepts, mappings and numerical inputs trigger dependent review. Review the entire affected public closure before republishing; avoid silently dropping qualifiers. Changes that may alter recommendations, cross-standard interpretations or scientific conclusions require domain review; until such review occurs, disclose the limitation and keep conclusions appropriately scoped.

Keep retrieval cutoff, evidence check date and substantive modification date distinct. Deployment time proves none of them. Release history binds the accepted catalog and content versions; later errata or withdrawal must be discoverable from old HTML/PDF release identity without changing the historical bytes. Public exports use only the reviewed publishable closure and do not contain private originals, draft conclusions or internal research artifacts.

## Acceptance and later work

Automated validation checks IDs, references, required reasoning, public dependency closure and update propagation. Substantive review checks source entailment, applicability, counterexamples, caveats and unintended normative claims. Browser review checks whether a reader can find this evidence. These checks are separate and none establishes environmental truth by itself.

Next content tasks are version-bound primary-source acquisition, expanded national/sector standards coverage, historical original sources, native reference cases for waste conventions, and a domain-reviewed functional equivalence case. The coverage inventory records these gaps without equating source counts with field-wide completeness.

## Historical numerical reconstruction

Separate reproduction of a source table's aggregation and units, a disclosed component calculation, and the original complete model. Fix the actual printed reference quantities and inspect table headings and cells before computing. Report gaps without inferring an error cause or tuning undisclosed inputs. A conditional bound on displayed-coefficient rounding does not cover undisclosed mass precision or empirical uncertainty. Bottle and package uses refer to different objects; comparing scenarios that change both does not isolate either one. New selected passages extend the existing work and reading identity; repeated carriers and independent arithmetic checks are not independent empirical data. Public exposition explains the actual calculations and limits; access attempts, review handling and remaining execution stay in development Markdown.

## Empirical calibration and probability meaning

Identify the sampled population element, sampling design, estimand and actual
reported statistic before assigning a distribution. Distinguish re-evaluation of
published summaries from rebuilding estimates using raw observations. A later
paper that reuses a dataset is not an independent empirical confirmation. Keep
source-internal numerical conflicts with their exact locations and unknown cause;
do not tune a missing input to make them agree.

For the pedigree illustration, separate a reference-group GSD ratio, an assumed
independent multiplicative component, and a squared-GSD representation. State the
additional assumptions when comparing these quantities. A hypothetical inventory
median and basic dispersion do not become source-derived measurements merely
because one additional factor has a published origin. Scoring reproducibility,
factor calibration, distribution coverage and domain applicability require
different evidence. The official scoring-test supplement extends the existing
work/reading identity; it does not establish correct scores, a training effect,
or effectiveness of the paper's newly proposed matrix.

## Evidence for an internal process and IO boundary

Record the work and its carrier separately when chapters of one thesis reprint
different papers. A numerical reproduction starts at the earliest supplied inputs.
For the Suh example, the displayed residual matrices permit a block solve. A
separate account-level reproduction requires unreduced supply, use and emissions
records, prices, period alignment and mappings. Retain conflicting price labels
with their original locations. Keep a published hypothetical comparison, an
author-designed counterexample, database price sensitivity and a reply available
only through its abstract in distinct evidence records.

For ecological-scarcity scores, trace a policy objective, the conversion to a critical annual load, the normalizing load and the published factor separately. Retain the baseline year and the factor edition when an estimate is revised. A ranking test requires the same service across alternatives; a change in the composition of one score supports a different claim. Independent checks distinguish exact reconstruction from published inputs, model sensitivity and observations of actual environmental change.

For a preference-based weight, trace the question actually shown to respondents, the unit of the estimated attribute coefficient and the reference unit of the published damage factor. Record whether the elicited outcome concerns one person, one household or a national total. Matching demographic tables establish common reported descriptors. Claiming identical microdata, deducing exclusions or substituting a later set of weights requires additional evidence. Distinguish arithmetic consistency with a reported number from a documented estimation procedure and an experiment on actual payments from the estimated price of a modelled loss.

For complete-study interpretation, identify which reported comparison serves the stated question and which missing data make it conditional. Review comments, author replies and the final artifact each require locators. An acceptance statement does not establish that every promised edit appears consistently in the released text. Behaviour frequencies with respondents as their denominator require an explicit mapping before being used as bag-weighted material substitutions.

For recovered-material chains, inspect physical processing and the point of substitution before applying quality factors. Keep burden attribution, modeled avoided activity and observed causal response separate. Compare fixed-service accounting choices with their actual assumptions; a changed reference efficiency prevents attributing the whole difference to one rule. Inspect the original correction alongside the original equation and distinguish a later stated rule from an inferred repair. A cycling experiment's stopping condition, sample removal, filler changes and omitted industrial operations must accompany any claim about material lifetime. Review the actual source table before inferring a grade mapping from generic categories.

For institutional application, read the provision's actor, version, modal force
and scope before tracing the chosen activity or output. Check the actual public
artifact and its review object separately. Historical rules, partial updates and
later transition guidance must not be collapsed into a single current method.
Defaults remain assumptions, and an observed reporting or activity relation does
not certify native implementation. Model comparisons need their own design,
units and actual applicability constraints; preserve a same-method requirement
when evaluating claims about country-specific methods and keep a regression's
predictive response separate from an intervention effect.

## Particulate pathway checks

Trace source emissions, atmospheric formation, receptor exposure, health
response and severity through their actual denominators. Check cohort exposure
assignment and adjustment separately from later baseline, age and outcome
choices. A WHO age standard does not replace the original risk population or
severity evidence. Equal factor names and units do not prove compatible
inhalation conventions, endpoints or reference states.

For response comparisons, name the working point, counterfactual and finite
interval. Distinguish parameter distributions from model, transfer and causal
uncertainty. For precursor chemistry, retain whether the perturbed quantity is
source emission or ambient total species and whether the output is particle
mass, inhaled mass or damage. A reduced model's comparison with its parent and a
field-constrained mechanism supply different evidence.

Inspect formal corrections alongside the original table and implementation.
Keep unresolved formula/table joins visible without changing source-reported
values. Source reviews, arithmetic, reader comprehension and actual publication
checks remain separate; none certifies an actual health intervention.

## Electricity service and response

Trace a delivery point through its actual grid activities before comparing
factors. Keep each segment's input/output loss denominator, plant/balancing-area
membership, clock convention and observed missing/zero values explicit. Separate
adjusted annual emissions, statistical estimands, original numerical model
responses and future capital scenarios. A native replay qualifies fixed code and
inputs only; a zero control or interpolation check does not establish an observed
intervention effect. Equal energy at different hours does not establish equal
final service. Inspect original release code, source data, actual versioned
outputs and technical documentation as different evidence layers. Preserve
unresolved printed/implemented distinctions and unpropagated uncertainty.

## Native exchange and format evidence

Keep original source records, migrated database objects, saved links, calculation
coordinates and reported totals as separate evidence. A readable binary marker
does not prove a current relational object exists. A saved edge can resolve while
its downstream treatment has no activity coordinate. Reference-flow type,
direction, signed amount and provider assignment must therefore be inspected
before describing preservation of a treatment service.

Bind native execution to the exact original archive, closed inspected database
tree, loaded modules and explicit target, parameter, allocation and method
settings. Independently derive expected source relationships before solving.
Retain source-model discrepancies after explaining their numerical residual;
an explained omission does not turn a failed original comparison into a pass.
Use quantity-appropriate tolerances, and distinguish treatment-direct inventory,
whole-system inventory of the same flow and a complete characterized total.

A modern module replay of a migrated example does not establish the historical
desktop version's behavior. Native format self-roundtrips retain their actual
extensions and export scope; a system-only LCI export cannot test method
preservation. Author-created zero/absent-factor controls examine retained
information and carry no environmental interpretation. Public exposition uses
minimal attributed source quantities and reviewed derived observations, while
complete originals, native exports and full data vectors remain private.

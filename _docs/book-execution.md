## First wave

Executable task: [knowledge-base #10](https://github.com/tiangong-lca/knowledge-base/issues/10).

Research questions:
1. What original/publicly inspectable source records can substantiate the formation and limits of early LCA frameworks, and which event identities remain unresolved?
2. How do foundational guides distinguish study purpose, functional unit/reference flow, boundary and study interpretation?
3. Which classic/community strata are missing or access-limited beyond the initial42 seed records?

Protocol: prioritize original official/institutional works and versions; record queries, dates, paths, rights and failures. Review selected passages with exact locators and surrounding qualifiers; do not relabel acquisition or metadata as full-book close reading. Separate original source assertions, retrospective testimony, normative authority and project synthesis. Scope-specific counterevidence and methodological appraisal accompany every promoted claim.

Raw originals remain outside Git in task-owned cache storage, with hashes and source locators in permitted public records. No raw full text or access credential enters the publication.

Current state: first-wave source-grounded manuscripts and public research infrastructure are implemented and undergoing integrated review/validation. The map now includes 70 distinct work/edition records after binding four additional primary reports to the 66-entry discovery set. Eight selected reading records represent six works; partial sections and overlapping locators are not whole-work completion. Four developing chapters contain 31 new canonical paragraphs, with 28 new evidence records. Whole-book scope and remaining gaps stay open. Final delivery evidence belongs in Issue #10 and the PR.

## Applied argument changes

The eight revisions in readings.json are now applied to the first-wave chapter structure and the goal's chapter briefs. Framework history distinguishes components/steps and event/report identity. The opening places integrative ambitions alongside omitted pathways and simplification. Service conditions precede quantity; reference-flow form is scoped to context. Comparability continues through model/data/interpretation. A useful study may stop short of a ranking; unknown differences do not establish equivalence. These applied editorial changes do not claim all affected units or source comparisons are finished.

Next work remains substantial: authorized ISO/SETAC originals and earliest reports; native-language community/source follow-up; allocation and recycling counterarguments; mathematical inventory structure; LCIA scientific models and releases; global data/standards; empirical cases and independent domain review. The programme remains active until the declared-edition gates are actually met.
